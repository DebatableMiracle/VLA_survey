% ===== 1. Replace the Data and Inference paragraphs of Section 7.1 with: =====
\textit{Data.} Items come from the DriveLM-nuScenes v1.1 training split~\cite{sima2024drivelm}. We evaluate 5 yes/no questions and 5 target-action questions, each drawn at random with seed 0 from items whose six camera images are available. Target-action answers are mapped to four classes (go straight, stationary, turn left, and turn right), and items in other classes are excluded. The six views are resized to $448\times252$ pixels and tiled into one $2\times3$ image; each model's processor then resizes this image to its native input resolution.

\textit{Inference.} Decoding is greedy, weights are in bf16, and the batch size is 1, on a single NVIDIA A100-SXM4-80GB GPU with PyTorch 2.5.1+cu121 and Transformers 5.18.0. Direct answers are limited to 64 new tokens and reasoning outputs to 512. A response without a valid label is scored incorrect. Latency is measured end to end (image preprocessing, prefill, and decoding, excluding tiling) after warm-up. Prompts are fixed across conditions and listed in the supplementary material.
